## Why measured plan costs are not yet a universal cost model

The calibration block's complete-request paired differences are:

| Difference | Mean | Conditional 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Decision plan minus native | -0.0046 ms | -0.0362 to 0.0363 ms |
| Count plan minus decision plan | 0.1339 ms | 0.0892 to 0.1684 ms |
| Ranked plan minus count plan | 0.0292 ms | 0.0074 to 0.0515 ms |
| Turnover plan minus ranked plan | 0.0234 ms | 0.0004 to 0.0463 ms |
| Four-comparison plan minus turnover plan | -0.0094 ms | -0.0327 to 0.0142 ms |

Negative and uncertain differences are retained. These are differences between compiled plans,
including dispatch, short-circuiting and cache effects; they are not pure operation prices.

The repeated native anchor averages **22.456 ms in the stop16/32 block**, versus
**14.641–15.594 ms in the other blocks**. The empty/native comparison also ranges from 0.33%
slower to 1.84% faster across its blocks, despite identical outputs and graph work. Reader/CPU
crossover protects within-block pairing; it does not make blocks interchangeable. The experiment
does not uniquely identify the source of this context dependence. Do not pool native anchors,
subtract old control gaps, or combine separately measured plan prices as universally additive
components. Preserve the observed costs rather than declare a measurement failure or rerun for
a preferred result. M2 must test the adequacy and uncertainty of any proposed surrogate.
