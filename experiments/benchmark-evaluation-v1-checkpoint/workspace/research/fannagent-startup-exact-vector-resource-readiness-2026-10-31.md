producer connection. The immutable reader origin, binding and state remain the

preflight before any restore, alongside original canonical parity and strict
interpreter/private-hook/library checks. All bindings restore on every exception.

A transparent framing delegate captures the original per-row identity accounting.
Independent replay requires one complete invocation, bounded bytes, correct
identity, metadata/delegate restoration and retained packed aliases at startup,
after both epochs and after all final verification scans.

appended. Expiry prevents further heavy verification and kills owned children

this finite acquisition without an automatic retry.
