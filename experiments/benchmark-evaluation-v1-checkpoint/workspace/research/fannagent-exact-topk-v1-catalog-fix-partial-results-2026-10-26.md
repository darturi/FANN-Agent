1. Reconcile the existing B2a compatibility matrix with this verified quality/cost evidence. Separate indexed ANN candidates from scans requiring distance work over all eligible rows. Retain current broad20%/50% predicate scope and unknown catalog/final-audit boundaries.

Add a prospectively declared cost-sanity/futility stage so an obviously expensive endpoint does not consume a full repetition schedule; it must not grant positive acceptance or retrospectively truncate this run.

The [pgvector0.8.6 filtering guidance](https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector/blob/v0.8.6/README.md#filtering) distinguishes selective exact indexes from approximate, partial and partitioned designs. That motivates a compatibility review, not a gain prediction for these broad predicates. PostgreSQL documents [bitmap ordering loss](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/indexes-bitmap-scans.html) and [partial-index predicate requirements](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/indexes-partial.html); these prevent treating a scalar index or bound partial target as a free drop-in.
