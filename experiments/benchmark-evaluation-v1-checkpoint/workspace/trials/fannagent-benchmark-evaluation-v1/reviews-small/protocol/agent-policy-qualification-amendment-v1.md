# Three-policy compiled development qualification and timing

This version implements the user's instruction to compile the three replay-eligible policies and prepare a Torch kit. It changes neither the frozen split nor the all-six-filter scope, common completion guard, graph, recall floor, zero-loss requirement, checkpoint semantics or final statistical criteria. Validation/test outcomes remain unopened. No provider calls are used.

The model-authored conservative_a, moderate_b_fixed and broad_c_fixed programs are compiled without threshold edits into new confirmation arms7/8/9. The new module is a copy of the previously qualified extended-effort engine, with a generated C rule include, predicate-identity accessor and extended arm bounds. Existing native, no-op, historical and forced arms retain their rules. Native and every policy are qualified against sealed development references under this new module before timing. Hexadecimal double constants preserve JSON values; missing turnover implies continue, as does fewer than ten qualifying candidates. No exact labels or query IDs enter runtime branches.

The common guard is byte-identical to the parent-qualified guard. Persistent ANN and exact readers use the same exported repeatable-read snapshot on a private restore. Native640 remains non-iterative on the same original graph. The initial and final policy outputs and chosen checkpoint must match the frozen full-precision replay for every case; fresh exact truth and live capture features must match the already qualified development evidence. Every final output must be complete, unique, finite and eligible, with no per-query recall loss and at least .95 mean recall in every condition.

The kit runs 450 development groups times seven conditions, four quality families (native and three policies), and two mirrored development timing epochs. Eight reader labels provide A/B copies of all four families, using the Williams base[0,1,7,2,6,3,5,4], rotated by group position/epoch/filter and reversed in epoch1. Group order is deterministically hashed using seed `reviews-agent-three-development-v1` and reversed in epoch1; filter order rotates. Each reader/filter/epoch has ten development warmups. The complete-request timer includes actual feature/branch overhead, continuation, decoding and fresh exact fallback. Setup/lookup build, attach, warmup and close are reported separately. All quality, timing and warmup output identities are retained.

The fixed workload is3,150 quality rows including9,450 compiled-policy cases,50,400 timed requests and1,120 warmups. Resource contract is4 physical CPUs/48GiB/8 hours and160,000 total ANN/exact attempts. This explicitly replaces the original qualification's smaller40,000-attempt/3-hour allowance for this versioned development experiment: two epochs across eight readers plus full live qualification require a larger bound. Worst-case dispatches, including exact fallback on every guarded request, remain below this cap. No automatic resubmission, candidate editing, additional allocation or provider request is included.

Use the existing equal-six-filter mean and agent ties (fewer comparisons/clauses, then canonical policy SHA256) after independent return verification. A single development allocation supplies selection measurements and A/B diagnostics, not confirmatory inference or a final practical-benefit claim. The final requirement remains a one-sided adjusted upper latency-ratio bound<=.95 under the sealed confirmatory design. Do not equate a favorable development mean or an observed stop with a speedup. The kit does not select a final policy, open validation/test or dispatch the final study; preserve negative/inconclusive results.

The binding seals the proposal pool, selected parent, split, policies, compiled module, source archive, expected replay outputs, queries and common guard. The return verifier reconstructs every record, timer and attempt and checks schedule and identity. Slurm COMPLETED0:0 remains an independent completion gate. Local C replay and synthetic PostgreSQL smoke are separate from original-graph qualification, which remains pending until the Torch return is verified.
