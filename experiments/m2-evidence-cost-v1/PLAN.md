## Two predeclared models

Targets are complete request ratios to each program's SAME-block native, averaged within
vector/filter/physical-CPU/cycle as appropriate. Native anchors from unrelated blocks are never
pooled. Per-vector bare-prefix/native ratios come only from each bare prefix's own block; these
already measured calibration bases are available on all exposed groups, including when checking
another program on that group. Therefore fold checks are conditional on this calibration library,
not a claim to predict latency on a never-measured future query or allocation.

Model A, signed additive plan proxy: use the observed bare stop16/32/64/native ratio as the search
base, or one for native continuation. Estimate per-filter count, ranked, turnover, decision and
comparison increments from complete-plan paired differences in calibration block3. Divide by
the corresponding observed operation counts, fit on four exposed folds, apply on the fifth.
Preserve negative estimates; do not clip them into positive costs. The complete-plan subtraction
is a hypothesis to test, not an assertion of isolated operation prices.

Model B, fixed ridge work/observation proxy: predict target ratio minus that same bare-prefix
base using normalized graph-vector and queue work plus per-checkpoint count/ranked/turnover
passes, actual decision calls and short-circuit comparison counts. Fit one model per filter,
with no intercept and fixed ridge penalty1e-3 on the mean squared error. Fixed feature scaling:
graph ratios minus one, passes/3, decision calls/3 and comparisons/12. No hyperparameter search,
threshold fitting, policy discovery or residual correction using held-program labels is allowed.
Report design rank and signed coefficients; zero or negative predicted total costs invalidate
that prediction. Feature arithmetic must reproduce measured work-pass counts on all v2 bindings.

Fit only calibration probes: decision/count/ranked/turnover/four-compare continue, count and
bare stop16/32/64. Empty/bypass and the legacy arms are diagnostics, not extra fit targets.
The six excluded complete programs are the three historical v2 transfers and their matched
continue controls. Neither their timing labels nor their outcomes fit coefficients. All seven
conditions are checked; unfiltered is reported separately from the primary equal-six-filter
objective. Exposed query folds only limit calibration fitting and diagnose stability.

- Absolute primary native-ratio error <=0.01 (one percentage point), in the five-fold
  out-of-fit predictions.
- The paired 20,000 exposed-vector bootstrap95% interval of signed primary ratio error lies
  wholly within[-0.01,+0.01]; seed20261116. This is conditional on the calibration library.
- Every condition's absolute ratio error <=0.02, including unfiltered; every exposed fold,
  physical CPU and cycle has primary absolute ratio error <=0.02.
- Preserve ordering of the three transfer programs whenever their observed primary ratios
  differ by more than0.01. Near ties do not establish precise relative ranking.

development shortlist guidance, with prediction uncertainty and unsupported programs explicitly
