## Decision rule and certificate

Only zero-loss, supported policies with at least one actual scalar early stop can be M5
nominees. Native remains an available comparator/abstention; no-stop controls and unsupported
policies stay in the report. Canonical duplicates consume submission slots but are deduplicated
for selection.

Enumerate every subset of size0..3 from at most9 submissions. For subset S, its conditional
regret bound is the positive part of the best selected upper bound (including native at1)
minus the best EXCLUDED lower bound (including native at1). Selecting all admissible nominees
has bound0. Accept only subsets with bound<=one native percentage point; prefer fewer live
nominees, then smaller bound, fewer comparisons/clauses and canonical hashes. Point estimates
never break overlapping-interval ties.

The certificate assumes costs lie inside the engineering intervals and ideal M5 measurement/
choice identifies the best available alternative. It does not include future measurement noise,
cross-allocation variation or unsupported programs. It is not a one-percent final-performance
guarantee. Actual M5 finalist/matched-control timings remain mandatory.

The selector matches exhaustive interval-corner oracle calculations over more than500 bounded
subset cases. Tests cover strict dominance, complexity ties, order invariance, deduplication,
native abstention, malformed intervals and no dependence on overlapping point estimates.
